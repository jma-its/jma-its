Slide 11

CPU vs RAM vs Storage

Study the slide panel first. Then read the web explanation.

CPU processes work, RAM is temporary workspace, and storage keeps Windows, programs, and files long-term. RAM and storage are not the same.

Think and answer

What is the difference between RAM and storage?

Show answer

RAM is temporary working memory; storage is long-term.

Safety rules

Study the slide panel first. Then read the web explanation.

System information tools are safe when used for reading. They become risky when students change settings, delete files, or share private data.

Think and answer

What is the beginner safety rule?

Show answer

Read first, protect data, and ask before changing anything.

Study the slide panel first. Then read the web explanation.

Do not share passwords, patient data, private files, or personal account details. Screenshots may accidentally reveal private information.

Think and answer

What should be checked before sending a screenshot?

Whether private or patient data is visible.

Slide 39

Do Not Change Settings Randomly

Study the slide panel first. Then read the web explanation.

Safe actions: open information windows, read names/status, take screenshots, write reports. Dangerous actions: disable devices, uninstall drivers, delete files, change unknown settings.

Think and answer

What is the classroom practice task?

Show answer

Observation and reporting, not repair.

Back to lesson map ↑
Slide 40

Do Not Delete Random Files

Study the slide panel first. Then read the web explanation.

Low storage does not mean delete anything you see. Do not delete Windows folders, Program Files, other people’s files, or unknown system files.

Think and answer

What is a safer first storage action?

Show answer

Report low space and ask before deleting important data.
