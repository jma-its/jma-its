Part 1 — Switches, binary numbers, and logic

Slide 5

Part 1 — The switch

Study the original slide first. Then read the web explanation.

A switch gives two clear states. OFF means an open circuit and can represent 0. ON means a closed circuit and can represent 1.

Think and answer

What physical states can represent 0 and 1?

Show answer

Open/OFF can represent 0; closed/ON can represent 1.

Slide 6

Why computers use two states

Study the original slide first. Then read the web explanation.

Computers use two states because machines need clear differences. A low-voltage range can mean 0 and a high-voltage range can mean 1. The exact values vary, but the category is clear.

Think and answer

Why are two states useful for machines?

Show answer

They create clear, reliable categories.

Slide 7

Why Computers Use Binary Numbers

Binary is a number system using only 0 and 1. It fits computers naturally because electronic switches also have two useful states: OFF and ON.

Think and answer

Slide 10

Binary Numbers with Switches

Study the original slide first. Then read the web explanation.

Four switches can represent the place values 8, 4, 2, and 1. An ON switch adds its value; an OFF switch adds nothing. For example, 0011 equals 2 + 1 = 3.

Think and answer

How do four switches show decimal 3?

Show answer

Turn ON the 2 and 1 switches: 0011.
