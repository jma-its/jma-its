JHTS 1.5 — START WITH TRAINING ACADEMY

1. Open academy.html on the class website.
2. Start Level 0. Do the Learn steps first, then Guided Practice.
3. Follow the instruction card. Use real menu pages, Search, View details and Confirm this patient.
4. Read patient IDs and dates of birth carefully. Looking at a record does not count as a mistake. Confirming the wrong patient does.
5. Finish Independent Practice and Skill Check. You need 80% or more and no critical identification mistakes to unlock Level 1 (unless your teacher changes class settings).
6. Do Level 1 the same way.
7. Open Save & Reports. Enter your name and download BOTH JSON and TXT.
8. Send both files to your teacher. Do NOT press Reset Data.

Use fictional training data only. Your progress is saved in this browser; do not clear browser data before exporting.
The language button switches English / Japanese without resetting the lesson.

JHTS 1.6 — LEVEL 2 APPOINTMENTS
After passing Level 1, open Academy → Level 2. Use View details on the Appointments page. Confirm only if the record is Scheduled and every ticket field matches; otherwise choose Needs review.
