My favorite place

In china is QingDao.Qingdao is such a chill and beautiful coastal city. It is famous for its signature look—"red roofs, green trees, blue seas, and azure skies." Walking through the old town feels so romantic, with all the cool century-old European architecture and the fresh sea breeze. When you go, you absolutely have to grab some fresh seafood and drink authentic Qingdao beer right out of a bag. The city has the perfect pace—it's got that modern energy, but it never loses that laid-back, cozy, seaside vibe. It is just a really healing place to be.

In Japan is Fukuoka.This is the first city I have lived in Japan for a long time.Fukuoka is such a chill and comfortable coastal city in Japan. It strikes the perfect balance between modern city life and a laid-back vibe, so the pace never feels overwhelming. The food scene here is absolutely amazing—the lively "Yatai" street food stalls have the best cozy energy, and the Hakata ramen and beef intestine hotpot are legendary. People here are so warm and welcoming, and since it's nestled between the mountains and the sea, you're never far from a great ocean view. It’s just one of those hidden gems you’ll want to visit again and again.
